Ocean Pollution

Why Ocean Pollution Matters

Plastic waste, chemicals and other pollutants can travel from streets and waterways into the ocean. They can injure marine animals, damage habitats and affect the health of ocean ecosystems.

Our mission is to raise awareness about the harmful effects of pollution on our oceans and marine life. We provide information on the causes, consequences, and prevention actions that can help protect our oceans for future generations.

Join us in making a difference for our oceans and the incredible marine life that calls them home.
Explore this site to learn about the effects of ocean pollution and practical ways to help prevent it. Small choices—such as using less single-use plastic and disposing of rubbish properly—can help keep pollution out of our waterways.
