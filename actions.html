Prevention Actions

  • Reduce plastic usage and recycle whenever possible.
  • Participate in beach clean-up events.
  • Support policies and initiatives that protect marine environments.
  • Educate others about the importance of ocean conservation.
  • Use eco-friendly products and avoid harmful chemicals that can pollute waterways.
  • Conserve water and reduce energy consumption to minimize environmental impact.
